## What are Agents?

Teli's agents are artificial intelligent employees that can call and text. Teli allows you to create and manage these AI agents to handle customer interactions more efficiently. Agents can be customized with different workflows, responses, and integrations to suit your business needs.

## Why Use Agents?

Agents in Teli AI provide several benefits:

- **Automated Responses** – Reduce response time by setting up AI-powered automation.
- **Personalized Interactions** – Customize responses based on customer data and history.
- **Scalability** – Easily manage multiple agents to handle large volumes of interactions.

## Creating an Agent

Follow these steps to create an agent in Teli AI:

1. **Open the AI Agents page** – Go to the **AI Agents** section from your Teli AI dashboard.
2. **Click "Create Agent"** – This opens the template selector.
3. **Choose a template or "Custom"** – Pick a template that fits your business needs, or select "Custom" to build one from scratch. Templates are available for both SMS and voice agents.
4. **Set the agent type and name** – Choose the **Agent Type** (SMS Agent or Voice Agent) and give your agent a name, then configure its voice, language, and prompt.
5. **Customize the agent** – Templated agents come with default parameters such as the prompt, voice, and emotions. Change each one to suit your needs, and use agent tools to give the agent abilities such as booking an appointment live during a call.
6. **Deploy and monitor** – Activate your agent and track performance through analytics.

## Agent Types

When you create an agent, you choose the **Agent Type** from a dropdown — **Voice Agent** or **SMS Agent**. Both templates and custom agents are available for each type.

### 1\. **Voice Agents**

Voice agents interact with customers via real-time phone calls.

### 2\. **SMS Agents**

SMS agents handle two-way texting automatically until the conversation reaches your predefined objective (e.g. ready to book a call, ready to purchase, or confirm an intent).
