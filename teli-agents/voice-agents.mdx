## What is a Voice Agent?

A Teli voice agent is an AI employee that places and answers real-time phone calls. You define its
persona, voice, and objective once, and it can hold natural conversations with your contacts —
qualifying leads, booking appointments, answering questions, or transferring warm calls to your team.

## Creating a voice agent

1. **Navigate to the Agents dashboard** – Open the Agents section from your Teli account.
2. **Click "Create Agent"** – Enter the agent's name, role, and primary function.
3. **Select a template or go custom** – Templated agents come pre-configured for common use cases;
   choose "Custom" to build your own from scratch. Unless indicated otherwise, templated agents are
   voice agents.
4. **Write the prompt** – Define the agent's persona, tone, and objective. You can let the built-in
   [AI prompt assistant](/teli-agents/ai-prompt-assistant) draft and refine this for you.
5. **Choose a voice** – Pick from Teli's voice library. By enabling the voice library integration
   you gain access to a wider variety of voices, and you can request a custom or cloned voice by
   contacting our team.
6. **Add dynamic variables (optional)** – Reference contact data directly in the script so each
   call is personalized. See [Using Dynamic Variables](/teli-agents/dynamic-variables2).
7. **Enable abilities (optional)** – Give the agent live capabilities such as checking a calendar
   and booking appointments during the call via native integrations.
8. **Test the agent** – Run a test call to hear how it sounds and confirm it stays on objective
   before assigning it to a campaign.

## Tips

- **Keep the objective clear.** A voice agent performs best when it has one well-defined goal.
- **Test with real scenarios.** Try common objections and edge cases on a test call.
- **Personalize with dynamic variables.** Referencing the contact's name and context makes calls
  feel natural.

Once your voice agent is ready, move on to
[Starting a Voice Campaign](/teli-agents/voice-campaign) to put it to work.
