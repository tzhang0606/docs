## What is a Voice Agent?

A Teli voice agent is an AI employee that places and answers real-time phone calls. You define its
persona, voice, and objective once, and it can hold natural conversations with your contacts —
qualifying leads, booking appointments, answering questions, or transferring warm calls to your team.

## Creating a voice agent

1. **Open the AI Agents page** – Open the **AI Agents** section from your Teli dashboard.
2. **Click "Create Agent"** – This opens the template selector.
3. **Choose a template or "Custom"** – Pick a template pre-configured for a common use case, or
   select "Custom" to build your own from scratch. Both voice and SMS templates are available.
4. **Set the agent type and name** – Choose **Voice Agent** as the agent type and give your agent
   a name.
5. **Write the prompt** – Define the agent's persona, tone, and objective. You can let the built-in
   [AI prompt assistant](/teli-agents/ai-prompt-assistant) draft and refine this for you.
6. **Choose a voice** – Pick from Teli's voice library. You can request a custom or cloned voice by
   contacting our team.
7. **Add dynamic variables (optional)** – Reference contact data directly in the script so each
   call is personalized. See [Using Dynamic Variables](/teli-agents/dynamic-variables2).
8. **Enable abilities (optional)** – Give the agent live capabilities such as checking a calendar
   and booking appointments during the call via agent tools.
9. **Test the agent** – Run a test call to hear how it sounds and confirm it stays on objective
   before assigning it to a campaign.

## Tips

- **Keep the objective clear.** A voice agent performs best when it has one well-defined goal.
- **Test with real scenarios.** Try common objections and edge cases on a test call.
- **Personalize with dynamic variables.** Referencing the contact's name and context makes calls
  feel natural.

Once your voice agent is ready, move on to
[Starting a Voice Campaign](/teli-agents/voice-campaign) to put it to work.
