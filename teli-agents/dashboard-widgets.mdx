## Your dashboard at a glance

The dashboard is the first thing you see when you log in. Its widgets give you a real-time,
high-level view of how your voice and SMS campaigns are performing — without having to dig into
individual campaigns.

## Common widgets

- **Call & message volume** – How many calls and texts have gone out over your selected time range.
- **Engagement** – Response and answer rates so you can see how contacts are reacting.
- **Objectives met** – How many conversations have reached your defined goal (booked, qualified,
  confirmed, etc.).
- **Active campaigns** – A snapshot of which campaigns are currently running.
- **Recent activity** – The latest calls and threads so you can spot what's happening right now.

## Customizing your dashboard

1. **Choose your time range** – Filter the widgets to today, this week, this month, or a custom
   range.
2. **Focus on the metrics that matter** – Arrange the widgets so the KPIs most important to your
   business are front and center.
3. **Drill in when you need detail** – The dashboard is for the big picture; when you need to
   monitor a specific area (like Round Robin), head to the
   [Analytics page](/teli-agents/analytics).

<Note>
  The dashboard is your overview. For deeper, campaign-level and Round Robin performance, use the
  [Analytics page](/teli-agents/analytics).
</Note>
