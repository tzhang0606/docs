## Your dashboard at a glance

The dashboard is your default landing page. It gives you a real-time, high-level view of how your SMS and voice campaigns are performing — built from widgets you can add and arrange to fit how you work.

## The widgets

You build your dashboard from a catalog of widgets, grouped by area:

- **SMS** – SMS Volume, Messages Over Time, Messages by Campaign, Response Rate, Qualified Leads, Deliverability Rate, and more.
- **Voice** – Total Calls, Call Volume Over Time, Call Sentiment, Success Rate, Average Call Duration, Calls by Agent.
- **Deal Flow** – Pipeline Funnel, Total Opportunities, Opportunities by User.
- **Round Robin** *(admin)* – assignments by user, inactivity rotations, org totals, and a loan-officer leaderboard.

Use **Add Widget** to pull any of these onto your dashboard, and **Reset Layout** to return to the default arrangement.

## Customizing your dashboard

1. **Choose your time range** – Use the date picker in the header. Presets include Today, Yesterday, Last 7 / 14 / 30 / 90 days, This month, and Last month, plus a custom range.
2. **Add the widgets you care about** – Open **Add Widget** and pick from the catalog.
3. **Arrange them** – Drag widgets by their handle to reorder and resize them, so the KPIs that matter most are front and center. Your layout is saved automatically.
4. **Drill in when you need detail** – The dashboard is your overview; for deeper campaign and Round Robin performance, head to the [Analytics page](/teli-agents/analytics).
