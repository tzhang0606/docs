## What is Round Robin?

Round Robin automatically rotates your campaign's outbound traffic across a pool of phone numbers
instead of sending everything from a single number. Spreading volume across multiple numbers
improves deliverability, keeps individual numbers within healthy sending limits, and reduces the
risk of a number being flagged for high volume.

## When to use it

- You're running a **high-volume** SMS or voice campaign.
- You want to **protect deliverability** by keeping per-number volume balanced.
- You have **multiple numbers** provisioned and want Teli to manage rotation for you.

## Activating Round Robin

1. **Open your campaign** – Go to the campaign you want to enable Round Robin on.
2. **Open campaign settings** – Find the Round Robin / number-pool option in the campaign
   configuration.
3. **Select the numbers to include** – Choose the pool of phone numbers Teli should rotate
   through. Each number should be provisioned and in good standing.
4. **Enable Round Robin** – Toggle Round Robin on. Teli will now distribute outbound messages and
   calls evenly across the selected numbers.
5. **Monitor performance** – Track how each number is performing from the
   [Analytics page](/teli-agents/analytics), which is where Round Robin performance is surfaced.

<Note>
  Setting up Round Robin the first time can involve number provisioning specific to your account.
  If you're not sure which numbers to pool, reach out to the Teli team and we'll help you configure it.
</Note>
