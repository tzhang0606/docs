## What is Round Robin?

Round Robin automatically assigns **qualified leads to your team members in rotation**. When a lead qualifies in a campaign, Teli hands it to the next user in the rotation instead of letting qualified conversations pile onto one person — so the workload is shared evenly across your team.

## When to use it

- You have a **team** handling qualified leads and want them distributed fairly.
- You want qualified conversations **auto-assigned** instead of manually divided up.
- You want to keep each rep's workload balanced, with the option to rotate stale leads to someone else.

<Note>
  Round Robin is an **admin-only** feature.
</Note>

## Activating Round Robin

1. **Open the Round Robin page** – In the sidebar (Admin section), open **Round Robin**.
2. **Select a campaign** – Click the campaign card you want to configure.
3. **Enable Round Robin** – Turn on the **Enable Round Robin** toggle. Qualified leads from that campaign will now be auto-assigned to users in rotation.
4. **Add users to the rotation** – Under **Users in Rotation**, click **Add** for each team member who should receive leads.
5. **(Optional) Inactivity Rotation** – Enable this so a lead is automatically rotated to the next user if the assigned user doesn't respond within a set timeout. Requires at least 2 users in the rotation.
6. **(Optional) Work Hours** – Set organization-wide work hours so leads are only assigned during those times.
7. **Save** – Click **Save Configuration**.

<Tip>
  You can also set up rotation while creating an SMS campaign — expand **"Set up rotation now"** in the campaign creation modal.
</Tip>

## Monitoring Round Robin

Round Robin performance is surfaced in two places:

- **Assignment Stats** on the Round Robin page — Round Robin vs. Manual vs. Rotated assignment counts.
- The **[Analytics page](/teli-agents/analytics)** — detailed per-user performance, campaign breakdowns, and a full rotation log.
