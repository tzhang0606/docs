## Going deeper than the dashboard

While the [dashboard widgets](/teli-agents/dashboard-widgets) give you a high-level overview, the
Analytics page is where you go for detailed, campaign-level performance — and it's the primary
place to **monitor Round Robin**.

## Monitoring Round Robin performance

When you run a campaign with [Round Robin](/teli-agents/round-robin) enabled, the Analytics page
breaks down performance across your pool of numbers so you can see how each one is doing:

- **Per-number volume** – How much traffic each number in the pool is sending.
- **Deliverability & engagement by number** – Spot any single number that's underperforming or
  getting flagged, so you can adjust the pool.
- **Balanced distribution** – Confirm that traffic is rotating evenly the way Round Robin intends.

## What else you'll find

- **Campaign performance** – Volume, response/answer rates, and objectives met per campaign.
- **Trends over time** – See how engagement changes across your chosen time range.
- **Filtering** – Narrow the view by campaign, number, or time period to answer specific questions.

## Using analytics to improve

1. **Check Round Robin balance** – Make sure no single number is overloaded or underused.
2. **Compare campaigns** – See which agents and scripts are driving the best outcomes.
3. **Act on the data** – Adjust your number pool, prompt, or targeting based on what the analytics
   show, then re-measure.
