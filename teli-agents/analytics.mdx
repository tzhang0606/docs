## Monitoring Round Robin performance

The Analytics page is where you monitor how **[Round Robin](/teli-agents/round-robin)** is performing across your team. It's an **admin-only** page, found under **Analytics** in the sidebar, and it's organized into a few tabs.

### LO Performance

See round-robin performance **per team member** — how many leads each user was assigned, their reply rate, how many leads rotated away from them, response speed, and current active workload. This is where you confirm the workload is balanced across your team.

### Campaigns

See which campaigns have Round Robin enabled, how many assignments were made by **Round Robin vs. manually**, and rotation counts per campaign.

### Rotation Log

A full audit trail of every assignment event — who received each lead, who it came from, when, and why. You can filter by phone, user, or source.

## Using analytics to improve

1. **Check workload balance** – Make sure no single team member is overloaded or sitting idle.
2. **Compare campaigns** – See which campaigns are driving the most qualified, assignable leads.
3. **Audit the rotation** – Use the Rotation Log to trace exactly how any lead was routed.
