## Campaign types

Teli Voice supports three campaign types for different use cases:

- **Inbound** – AI agents accept inbound calls 24/7. Best for customer service and receptionists.
- **Bulk Outbound** – AI agents reach thousands of contacts within minutes. Best for call centers,
  lead qualification, and sales.
- **Gradual Live Transfer** – AI agents call a set number of contacts at once and transfer warm
  conversations to a human agent. Best for human-in-the-loop use cases like mortgage and insurance.

## Starting an Inbound campaign

1. **Deploy an inbound agent** – Create or select the [voice agent](/teli-agents/voice-agents) you
   want to answer calls.
2. **Create a campaign** – In Teli Voice, create a new campaign, name it, and select the **Inbound**
   type.
3. **Purchase a phone number** – Each campaign needs its own dedicated Teli number. Select an
   unused number and activate it.
4. **Assign your agent** – Attach the agent you created and activate it. Your number is now ready
   to accept inbound calls.
5. **Reference contact data (optional)** – Supply a contact group so the agent recognizes callers
   and can hold a personalized conversation.

## Starting a Bulk Outbound campaign

1. **Deploy an outbound agent** – Create or select your voice agent.
2. **Create a campaign** – Create a new campaign and select the **Bulk Outbound** type.
3. **Purchase a phone number** – Select and activate a dedicated number for the campaign.
4. **Assign your agent** – Attach and activate the agent. You can run a test call from the
   assignment window.
5. **Select lead group and start** – Choose the contact group to dial, confirm the contacts in the
   preview, then start the campaign. Results populate under the call history tab.

## Starting a Gradual Live Transfer campaign

1. **Deploy an outbound agent** – Create or select your voice agent.
2. **Create a campaign** – Create a new campaign and select the **Gradual Live Transfer** type.
3. **Purchase a phone number** – Select and activate a dedicated number.
4. **Assign your agent** – Attach and activate the agent (test calls available from the same window).
5. **Invite your team** – Live transfers require human team members. Invite them from your profile
   under the organization invitation tab; once they have active Teli accounts you can assign them to
   the campaign.
6. **Configure transfers and start** – Select the lead group, confirm the contacts, set how many
   calls go out at once, and assign your team to accept transfers. When a team member accepts a
   call they're marked unavailable until they confirm the call is over; when everyone is busy, the
   dialer pauses automatically before the next batch.

<Note>
  In the campaign modal you can optionally select a **Number Pool** for area-code matching, so
  outbound calls show a caller ID that matches the contact's area code.
</Note>
